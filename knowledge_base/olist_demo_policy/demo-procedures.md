These procedures apply only to this independent research application. They are
demonstration assumptions, not Olist policies or promises from a merchant.

Choose a curated historical demo account before looking up transactions. Public
references refer only to records associated with that account. Historical
purchase, payment, shipping and review records cannot prove current eligibility
for a return, refund or warranty claim. No refunds, cancellations, delivery
changes, seller contact or legal determinations can be executed here.

Case-specific questions beyond verified sources require human review. A saved
request is for this application's staff inbox, not official Olist support. No
response time, merchant response or legal outcome is promised. Review comments
are unavailable pending separate privacy validation. Missing facts stay unknown.
