# Warranty

Hardware products carry a one-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. The
warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover accidental damage,
normal wear, or damage from misuse.

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order ID and a description of
the problem. If the claim is covered, we repair or replace the item at no charge,
including shipping.

The warranty is non-transferable and applies to the original purchaser. Keep your
order confirmation as proof of the delivery date.

Accidental damage is not covered by the standard warranty. Some products offer a
separate accidental-damage plan at purchase; if you bought one, its terms are in
your account under Protection Plans.
