# Billing

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same day each month. You can see your
next renewal date and change your plan from the Billing page in your account.

To cancel a subscription, go to the Billing page and choose Cancel. Cancellation
takes effect at the end of the current billing period; you keep access until then
and are not charged again.

If you were charged after cancelling, contact support with your account email and
the charge date. Verified duplicate or post-cancellation charges are refunded to
the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

We accept major credit cards. We never ask for your full card number over chat or
email. To update a card, use the secure Billing page in your account.
