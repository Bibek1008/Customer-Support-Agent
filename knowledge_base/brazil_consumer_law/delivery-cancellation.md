Delivery and cancellation: Articles 30 and 35 address binding offers and remedies
when suppliers refuse fulfillment, including performance, equivalent supply or
rescission with repayment and possible damages.

These summaries cannot determine an individual historical claim. Seek qualified
consumer assistance or human review; transaction timestamps alone do not establish
