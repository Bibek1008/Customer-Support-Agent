Defective products: Article 18 generally allows repair within thirty days,
followed by replacement, reimbursement or proportional reduction; statutory
exceptions can permit immediate remedies. Article 26 provides thirty days for
apparent defects in non-durable products/services and ninety for durable ones;
hidden-defect timing starts when discovered. Complaints and other circumstances
can affect deadlines. Article 24 establishes a legal guarantee independent of
written terms; Article 50 makes contractual warranties supplementary.

These summaries cannot determine an individual historical claim. Seek qualified
consumer assistance or human review; transaction timestamps alone do not establish
eligibility. No merchant-specific refund timing or warranty terms are verified.
