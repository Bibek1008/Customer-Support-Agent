General Brazilian legal information, not official Olist terms. Article 2 requires
clear supplier, product, price, payment, availability and delivery information.
Article 4 requires electronic customer service for information, complaints,
suspension and cancellation, immediate receipt confirmation and a supplier
response within five days. This is a legal requirement, not this demo's service promise.

Return rights and refunds: Article 5 addresses withdrawal channels, including the
purchase tool; accessory contracts end without consumer charges. The supplier
must promptly notify the financial institution to prevent a charge or reverse a
posted charge and confirm receipt of withdrawal. It does not establish a
merchant-specific bank processing schedule.

Article 6 requires fulfillment of delivery terms, quantity, quality and suitability.
Individual cancellation, refund, delivery and complaint outcomes require review
of facts and applicable law. This application cannot send a complaint to a real
merchant, issue a refund or guarantee an outcome. Offer qualified human review
where these sources do not settle the individual case.
