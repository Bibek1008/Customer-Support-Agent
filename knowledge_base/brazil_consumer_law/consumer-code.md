General Brazilian law, not Olist company policy. Article 49 provides seven days
from signature or receipt for withdrawal from qualifying off-premises contracts,
with immediate, inflation-adjusted return of amounts paid during that period.

These summaries cannot determine an individual historical claim. Seek qualified
consumer assistance or human review; transaction timestamps alone do not establish
eligibility. No merchant-specific refund timing or warranty terms are verified.
