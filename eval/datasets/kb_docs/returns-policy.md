# Returns and Refunds

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item
must be unused and in its original packaging. Start a return from the Orders page
in your account, or ask support and we will create a return label for you.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days
after we receive the returned item. You will get an email when the refund is
processed.

Some items are final sale and cannot be returned: gift cards, downloadable
software, and clearance items marked "final sale". These are noted on the product
page before purchase.

If an item arrives damaged or defective, you do not need to pay for return
shipping. Report it within 30 days and we will send a prepaid label and a
replacement or refund.
