# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United
States. Expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM
local time ship the same business day.

We ship with UPS and FedEx. The carrier and a tracking number appear on your
order once it leaves our warehouse. You can check the status of any order by its
order ID.

We do not ship on weekends or public holidays. An order placed on a Friday
afternoon with standard shipping typically arrives the following week.

International shipping is available to select countries and takes 7 to 14 business
days, plus any customs processing time. Import duties are the customer's
responsibility.
